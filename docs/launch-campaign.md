# Radiant Life Balance launch kit

Use one clear call to action everywhere: **Take the free 30-question quiz at radiantlifebalance.com/quiz**. Avoid claims that the quiz diagnoses a condition. Add a short `?ref=creatorname` tag to the quiz link for each collaborator. Check `/metrics` for campaign totals and Google Analytics for site traffic. Ask permission before using a person's results as a testimonial.

## Creator outreach drafts

Personalize the first sentence with a real reason you follow each creator. Do not mass-send identical messages. Offer a tagged link so both sides can see quiz visits.

1. **Wellness educator:** I liked your recent post about sustainable routines. I built a free six-area life balance quiz that gives people a gentle first step. Would you consider trying it and sharing it if it fits your audience?
2. **Relationship educator:** Your work on better conversations overlaps with one area in my free Life Balance assessment. Could I send you a link to review and, if useful, share?
3. **Personal finance educator:** The Money section is part of a broader six-area self-assessment. It may help people see where money stress connects with other priorities. Would you like a preview?
4. **Faith community leader:** This free quiz includes purpose, relationships and everyday habits without requiring people to share results publicly. Would it be useful for your community?
5. **Podcast host:** I would be glad to discuss how a whole-life assessment helps people identify one workable next step. May I send you the quiz and a short topic outline?
6. **Book club organizer:** Members could each take the quiz and discuss which strength might support a growth area. Would that be a useful opening activity?
7. **Fitness coach:** My short quiz places physical habits alongside other life priorities. I think your clients might appreciate the context. Would you be open to trying it?
8. **Career coach:** Direction and purpose are part of our six-area assessment. Could I send it for your honest feedback?
9. **Newsletter writer:** I thought of your readers because of your recent piece on small changes. If the free quiz resonates, I'd love for you to include it in a future issue.
10. **Community organizer:** Could the free quiz and 30-day challenge support a group discussion? I'd be happy to provide a specific link for your group.

## First 100 users

Ask ten people you already know to take the free quiz, then ask what surprised them and where the flow was confusing. Invite each to share with one other person only if it helped them. Test mobile and desktop. Track quiz starts, completions and shares weekly. Record recurring feedback before buying ads. Use your own example report to demonstrate the optional paid PDF without promising outcomes.

## Six-email Kit series (draft; send only to opted-in subscribers)

1. **Welcome: Your next small step** — Thank you for taking the quiz. Your scores are a starting point. Pick the lowest area you want to work on and choose one five-minute action. Return to your results when you are ready. CTA: View your results.
2. **The strength you can borrow** — Look at your strongest area. What habit, support or mindset helped you there? Apply one part of it to your first priority. CTA: Explore that area.
3. **Make the change easier** — Replace a big goal with a specific cue and a very small action. Try it at the same time three days in a row. CTA: Start the 30-day challenge.
4. **Bring someone with you** — A friend or partner can help you notice progress without judging your scores. Share the free quiz if that feels useful. CTA: Invite a friend.
5. **Go deeper where it matters** — The free deep-dive quizzes identify subareas in your two biggest priorities. CTA: Try a deep dive.
6. **Review and decide** — What has changed in the last month? Keep one habit, adjust another, and consider retaking the quiz later. Optional: a paid personalized PDF can connect your strengths and priorities. CTA: Return to your results.
